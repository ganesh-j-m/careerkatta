🚀 Career Katta: Better Education, Rural Growth & a Brighter Future

An official, responsive reporting and resource portal designed to showcase the progress and impact of Career Katta—a pioneering initiative of the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra, in association with MITSC (Maharashtra Information Technology Support Center).

🚀 Live Project Link: https://ganesh-j-m.github.io/careerkatta/

🌟 Key Project Highlights

🎯 Core Mission Showcase: Highlights the journey of bringing top-tier career guidance, skill development, and tech training straight to villages and small-town colleges.

📚 NEP 2020 Integration: Spotlights how the initiative drives structural educational reforms in Maharashtra as aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020).

💡 Value-Driven Foundation: Dedicated sections detailing the core inspirations behind Career Katta—Swami Vivekananda's youth empowerment values and Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's vision of technology-driven nation-building.

🎨 Interactive Floating Card UI: Features a modern overlap of event images (like award presentations and large-scale youth seminars) giving visitors a quick, visually engaging look at the live activities.

📱 Responsive Layout: Crafted with precision utilizing Bootstrap 5 grid alignments to provide seamless navigation on any screen size.

🛠️ Tech Stack Used

Markup & Layout: HTML5 & CSS3

CSS Framework: Bootstrap 5 (Responsive containers & flexbox alignments)

Typography: Google Fonts (Poppins & Khand)

Official Branding: Integrated institutional logos of the Maharashtra Government Higher & Technical Education Dept. and MITSC.
